Zumi Waves

Circles

Skill Drops

Unique to Zumi

Wave Challenges

Post a challenge, fund a Drops prize pool, let your community submit Wave entries, and let everyone vote. The winner takes the pool. Native virality built into the platform — no third-party tools, no workarounds. One challenge can bring thousands of new eyes to your Circle.

Gated Rooms

Live or async audio/video rooms. Set an entry price in Drops. Host AMAs, workshops, and private listening sessions.

Flow Market

A creator-to-creator gig economy. Post a collab request, pay in Drops. Build together, earn together.

Circle Insights

Full analytics dashboard for Circle owners. Member growth, Drops revenue, Wave performance, churn — all in one place.
